# Retail-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard
This project involved analyzing retail sales data to identify sales trends, evaluate business performance, compare product category performance, and determine the factors influencing revenue generation.
### 1. Project Objectives
* Analyze average sales performance across states and years.
* Identify the top five suburbs by total sales.
* Evaluate average sales performance across product categories.
* Identify the highest-performing sales managers.
* Analyze sales performance by day of the week.
* Determine monthly sales contributions and seasonal trends.
* Develop key performance indicators (KPIs) for monitoring business performance.
* Provide actionable recommendations to improve sales and business operations.
### 3. Business Problem
Understanding sales performance and identifying opportunities to improve revenue
#### 4.	Dataset
Retail sales transaction data containing 76,945 rows in the source worksheet, including instruction rows; the KPI summary reports 76,930 sales records. 
### 5. Tools Used
1. Microsoft Excel: Data analysis, data organization, calculations, and reporting.
2. PivotTables: Sales aggregation, category comparisons, and performance analysis.
3. Excel Charts: Visual representation of sales trends and comparisons.
4. KPI Analysis: Evaluation of average sales, minimum sales, maximum sales, transaction count, and total revenue.
5. Dashboard Reporting: Presentation of sales performance metrics and analytical findings.
### 6. Analysis Performed
Geographic sales analysis, product category analysis, sales manager ranking, weekday analysis, and monthly sales contribution. 
### 7.	Key Findings and Insights
* Total Sales: The dataset recorded approximately 57.67 million in total sales across 76,930 sales records.
* Average Sales: Average sales per record were approximately 749.59.
* State Performance: New South Wales (NSW) recorded the highest average sales at approximately 916.48, while the Australian Capital Territory (ACT) recorded the lowest at approximately 408.68.
* Suburb Performance: Campbelltown recorded the highest total sales among the five suburbs analyzed, generating approximately 1.72 million.
* Product Category Performance: Shoes recorded the highest average sales among the categories analyzed, at approximately 1,188.88, followed by men's products at approximately 1,072.86.
* Sales Manager Performance: John Gardner ranked first among the ten managers analyzed, with approximately 4.81 million in total sales.
* Day-of-Week Performance: Thursday recorded the highest combined sales among the weekdays shown in the analysis, at approximately 10.18 million.
* Monthly Sales Trends: August contributed approximately 11.49% of total sales, followed by March at approximately 11.40%. October recorded the lowest monthly contribution, at approximately 4.59%.
### 8.	Recommendations 
* Investigate the factors contributing to strong sales performance in NSW and replicate successful strategies in lower-performing states.
* Develop targeted promotions and customer engagement strategies for underperforming suburbs.
* Review the performance of product categories to identify opportunities for cross-selling, upselling, and inventory optimization.
* Study the sales techniques used by high-performing managers and share effective practices across the sales team.
* Use historical monthly sales patterns to improve inventory planning, promotional scheduling, and resource allocation.
* Investigate the reasons for lower sales contributions in October, November, and December before developing targeted improvement strategies.
* Establish a regular sales dashboard reporting process to monitor performance and support
